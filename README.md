# goal_tracker
A responsive goal tracker app that let users create their own gaols, edit, pause/resume and complete their goals in a funny way.
used MUI for theme and style,and i18next for language. hope enjoy the app.☺️
---
 feature:
 
🌸Dashboard: <br><br>
  ✨ shows your level,xp,streak,goals,completed and completion percentage. <br>
  📊chart ---> weekly activity and categories<br>
   every goal you create appear here.<br>
   
Goal card:
1. progress button (complete step by step (till your target )
2. pause/resume button 
3. complete action button (complete your goal)
4. edit button (edit your title, category or target )
5. details button (more info about your card )
6. delete icon <br>
   🍀 Each card shows: <br>
    1. title<br>
   2.category <br>
   3.status <br>
   4.progressbar <br>
---
🌸Goals: <br>
1.Filter goals <br>
2.All goals  <br>
3.Active goals <br>
4.Paused goals <br>
5.Completed goals <br>
6.Archive goals <br>

🌸Achievements: <br>

 🍀unlock: <br>
1 goal = "first goal" <br>
5 goals = "goal master"<br><br>

 🍀completion:<br>
1 goal = "first completed"<br>
10 goals = "goal crusher"<br><br>

 🍀xp:<br>
100 xp = level 2 <br>
each progress = 10 xp <br>
500 xp = level 5 <br> <br>

 🍀streak: <br>
7 days streak <br>
30 days streak <br>
100 days streak <br>
